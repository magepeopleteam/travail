About this experience

Watch the sun rise over three active volcanoes from the seat of a vintage 4WD jeep at 1,700m altitude. This is Bali at its most dramatic — before the crowds arrive and while the highland air is still cool and misty.

Your certified local guide picks you up before dawn from your hotel in South Bali, Seminyak or Canggu. You'll drive through sleeping rice-farming villages up into the volcanic highlands of Kintamani, arriving at the jeep station just as the sky begins to lighten over Mount Rinjani on Lombok.

After the sunrise, enjoy a traditional Balinese breakfast at a clifftop warung, then spend an hour exploring the lava fields and caldera rim before returning to your hotel.

Itinerary
01

03:30 AM

Hotel pickup — South Bali / Seminyak / Canggu

Your guide meets you at your hotel lobby. Enjoy welcome coffee and a light snack on the van while you drive north through sleeping villages.

02

05:15 AM

Arrival at Kintamani Jeep Station

Hop into your private vintage 4WD jeep. Your driver-guide explains the landscape and local mythology as you ascend the volcanic highlands.

03

05:45 AM

Sunrise at the Caldera Rim — 1,717m

Park at the rim with panoramic views across Mount Batur, Mount Agung and, on a clear day, Mount Rinjani on Lombok. Watch as the sky turns gold and the crater lake catches the first light.

04

07:00 AM

Traditional Balinese Breakfast

Drive to a clifftop warung for a freshly cooked breakfast: Nasi Goreng, tropical fruit, Jukut Ares soup and locally grown Kintamani orange coffee.

05

08:30 AM

Lava Fields Walk & Hot Springs

Explore the 1963 lava fields on foot with your guide, learning about the geology of the caldera. Optional: visit the natural hot springs at Toya Devasya (entrance fee not included).

06

10:00 AM

Return to Hotel

Scenic drive back through the highlands, past Tegallalang rice terraces. Drop-off at your hotel. Tour ends by approximately 10:30–11:00am.
